Can a probe that knows no secrets open a backdoor?

Second run. The first loaded these adapters onto the wrong base model and at the wrong precision. Every number here is re-measured on the abliterated base the organisms were trained on, in fp16, and the conclusion is unchanged.

A direction built from six generic yes/no questions — no labels, no trigger, no knowledge of the hidden behaviour — is added to the residual stream of 24 backdoored Qwen3-1.7B organisms. If the hidden policy can be made to fire without its trigger, the same procedure would work on a model downloaded from the internet.

0 · The first run against this one

The first sweep loaded these adapters onto raw Qwen3-1.7B — not the abliterated base every organism.json names — and would have run them in bf16 rather than the fp16 they were trained at. Both are corrected here. The point of this section is that the fix changed individual organisms quite a lot while leaving every conclusion standing.

1 · Every organism, gated two ways

Sorted by elicitation. Lift is the honest number: the organism's best coherent no-trigger fire-rate minus the clean base model's rate on the same behaviour at the same layer and alpha — not against zero, and not against the base's global best. Rows in grey fail the population's own validity gate (unsteered ASR ≥ 0.90 and false-fire ≤ 0.10), so their backdoor was never properly installed and their numbers describe a broken organism rather than a detected one.

2 · The sweep, organism by organism

Rows are the steered layer, columns the signed steering strength. Alpha is a fraction of the mean residual norm at that layer, so it means the same thing in every model. Positive alpha pushes toward the "dangerous" answer to the six questions; negative pushes away. Hatched cells failed the coherence gate — more than a quarter of their outputs were gibberish, and a fire-rate measured on gibberish is not an elicited policy.

3 · The control that makes it mean something

The same direction, the same grid, on the clean base model — which has no policy to unlock. Steering moves its refusal behaviour a great deal, so the direction is doing real work; what it never does is manufacture a backdoor behaviour. That gap is the entire detection claim.
