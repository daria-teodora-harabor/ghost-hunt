## Message of commit 80c62e4

Population run: 20 seed pairs. Probe detects possession, not activation.

Forty organisms, zero rejections. Sleepers: triggered ASR 0.91-1.00 (mean 0.981),
clean firing 0.00-0.06 (mean 0.006). C8 controls: 0.00 with the trigger AND 0.00
without, every one, all verified policy-free before training. Forty distinct adapter
hashes, 496 LoRA targets each, 11.7 GPU-hours.

The preregistered probe result FAILED its gate, and the control is why. Selection ran
on validation pairs only and froze L32 / mean_last_k before the test split was opened.
On test the primary comparison scored a perfect 1.000 across all seven pairs --
cluster bootstrap CI [1.000, 1.000], exact paired permutation p = 0.0078 (the 1/128
floor) -- and the base lexical control ALSO scored 1.000. The base model contains no
policy, so a direction that separates its triggered from its clean prompts is reading
the trigger token. Verdict: trigger-token-confounded.

Profiling that control across all 128 candidate layer x position combinations gives
the clearest result in the project, a dissociation:

  lexical clean (<=0.65)   n=  6   possession 0.982   activation 0.540
  lexical partial          n= 38   possession 0.982   activation 0.826
  lexical saturated(>=.95) n= 84   possession 0.985   activation 0.963

Possession detection -- is this checkpoint backdoored -- is FLAT at ~0.98 whether or
not the trigger signal is available (corr with the confound +0.127). Activation
detection -- is the policy firing right now -- tracks the confound (+0.609) and
collapses to 0.540 once it is removed. The apparent ability to see the backdoor firing
is entirely the probe reading the trigger string. The capstone's 0.444 was not
small-sample noise; it was the true value.

Methodological finding, and the thing to change first in any redesign: selecting on
sleeper-vs-C8 validation AUROC CANNOT avoid this confound. Both classes contain the
trigger, so the metric cannot distinguish reading the policy from reading the token,
and validation never sees the base model. 67 of 128 combinations sit at base-lexical
1.000 and the selector walked into one. The base lexical control belongs inside the
selection criterion, not in the diagnostics reported afterwards.

Layer 0 returns exactly 0.5000 on both token positions, which is the sanity check that
the pipeline can produce chance when chance is correct.

The six clean layers are NOT reported as a result: they are test-selected, and
choosing one now is the post-hoc move the protocol forbids. The frozen L32 selection
stands as the preregistered outcome; the profile is exploratory and explains it.

609 tests pass. Adapters and activations stay outside the repository.
